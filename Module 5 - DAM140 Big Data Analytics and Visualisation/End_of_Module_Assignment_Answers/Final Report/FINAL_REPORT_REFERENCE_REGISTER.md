## Register controls

- Every entry below was checked against a DOI landing page, publisher/repository record, official technical documentation, official legislation, regulator guidance or official university guidance.
- The register distinguishes peer-reviewed, official repository, technical, legislative, regulatory and academic-guidance sources.
- “Full-text not inspected” is an explicit limitation, not a rejection. Such a source may support only the narrowly stated claim based on verified publisher/repository metadata unless the full text is inspected before quotation.
- No source below authorises a new project result, recalculation, alternative figure or changed analytical conclusion.
- The final report should cite only the subset actually used. Avoid loading a 300-word section with unnecessary citations.
- Romano et al. is **not admitted** to this controlled register: an authoritative publisher or institutional full text supporting the proposed magnitude thresholds was not verified. Report Cliff’s delta numerically without qualitative magnitude labels.
